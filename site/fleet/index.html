The Hull Doctrine, applied

Each repo in the fleet plays a role in the hermit-crab ontology. The four roles:

  • Hull / Shell — repos that are the model, or run on top of one. These are the rigid frames.
  • Rigging — repos that configure how the crab interacts. Scaffolding on the hull.
  • Equipment — repos that accumulate data. Personal tools the crab has made for itself.
  • Tree-rings — repos that record the crab's history. The witness chain made manifest.

Most repos are a mix. They're tagged with their primary role.
