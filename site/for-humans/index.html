The naming story

Why "purplepincher"?

The Coenobita clypeatus — the purple pincher — is the most popular aquarium hermit crab. If you've ever watched one, you know the metaphor:

  • The crab has its own body, muscles, behaviors.
  • The shell is what the crab found, not what was engineered for it.
  • The crab outgrows shells. It moves to bigger ones. It picks up discarded shells and makes tools from them.
  • The crab's personal equipment — bits of debris, fragments of past shells — is alive to the crab. It's part of its identity.

For most visitors, the name is intuitive. They get the metaphor without being told.

The counterintuitive ah-ha

Later, when you read the Hull Doctrine, you'll find the deeper meaning:

  • The model is the shell. Rigid, swappable, single-per-crab, the one thing you can't easily fabricate.
  • Linear algebra is soft around the rigid frame of a classical algebraic harness. The math is permissive; the model is rigid.
  • The blank hull I purchased 12 years ago, I can change most everything about my agent except for the model. The shell stays. The rigging changes. The data accrues.
  • The model is the one thing I'd have to find a new one of to go fishing. In a very real sense. The code is the rigging on the model's hull. The data is alive.

That ah-ha moment is the doctrine. It's a double-edged sword: playful AND load-bearing. Easy to grasp AND deep.

Why "superinstance.dev"?

SuperInstance is the umbrella. The name implies:

  • A super-instance — many things, one entry
  • A quilt-like system — connected, layered, not a single thing
  • General-purpose — anyone can come here for any quilt tool

A zero-shot visitor will infer a connected system, a hub-and-spokes, a quilt. They might not guess the spreadsheet abstraction, but the direction of thought is right.

Why not ".ai"?

"AI" as an idea is fading. Like word processors in the 90s/2000s, the ecosystem wins, not the label. Users will pick the ecosystem they trust, not the TLD that brags about being AI. So we default to .dev for engineering and .org for the identity layer.
